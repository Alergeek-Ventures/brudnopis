## Overview

Brudnopis is a handwritten note-taking application. We believe in complete data
privacy — this app **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
data whatsoever**.

## Data Collection

We do **not** collect any of the following:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Device identifiers or hardware information
- Location data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports
- Cookies or tracking data

All notes and data you create in the app remain **entirely on your device**.

## Third-Party Services

Brudnopis does **not** integrate any third-party services that collect user data.
There are no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporting tools, or
cloud services embedded in this app.

## Data Sharing

Since we do not collect any data, we have **nothing to share** with third
parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

All your data is stored locally on your device. We have no access to it. You can
delete your data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings
or by uninstalling the app.

## Children's Privacy

This app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
and similar regulations by design — no data is collected from any user regardless
of age.

## Security

Since no data is transmitted from your device, there is no risk of data breach
from our side. Your notes remain on your device and are subject to your device's
own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.).
